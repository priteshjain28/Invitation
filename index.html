How it happened

Our story, in six chapters

2022

Two suitcases. One campus.

Two students crossed continents for their Master's at the University of Maryland. Neither of us knew we'd be taking home much more than a degree.

2023

Same playlist. Same people.

From cooking together and chasing sunsets to endless road trips and matching playlists — we kept discovering that our favourite things looked surprisingly alike.

2024

Caps off. Game on.

We graduated, chased big dreams, applied for countless jobs, celebrated every little win, and stood by each other through every setback. Every challenge became a little easier because we faced it together.

2025

Miles couldn't compete.

He moved to Arizona. She stayed in Virginia. Different states, countless flights, endless FaceTime calls — and somehow, the distance only made us stronger.

2026 · May

The families met.

Tea was poured. Stories were shared. Smiles came easily. Two families met — and it instantly felt like one.

2026 · November

And now — you.

Every beautiful story deserves its favourite people in the final chapter. Come celebrate with us as we make it official.
